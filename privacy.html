Privacy policy

dropyblox automation. Last updated November 2, 2026.

What this is

dropyblox automation is a private tool run by the owner of the dropyblox YouTube channel, on the owner's own computer. It is the only account it is used with.

YouTube API Services

This tool uses the YouTube API Services. Using it means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the tool reads and writes the authorized channel's own data: video files and their metadata (titles, descriptions, privacy and schedule settings), playlists, comments on the channel's videos, and the channel's YouTube Analytics reports. It may also read public YouTube data, such as public video statistics.

How the data is used

To upload and schedule the channel's videos, edit their metadata, reply to comments, and produce private performance reports for the channel owner. The data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Storage and retention

Access tokens and reports are stored only on the channel owner's computer. Data from the channel's own account is kept while the owner uses the tool and deleted when access is revoked. Public data from other channels is kept for at most 30 days unless refreshed. This website sets no cookies and has no tracking.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time on the Google security settings page; the tool then deletes its stored tokens.
